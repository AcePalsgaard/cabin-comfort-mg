First release of Cabin Comfort for MG:
• Warm up and Chill profiles: heat, defrost, seats, wheel and rear window in one tap
• Keeps the A/C going for up to 4 runs (about 40 min)
• Suggests a profile from the outside temperature
• Home screen widgets: Auto button and your two profiles
• Your car drawn in its own colour, or use your own photo
• Commands are retried in the background when MG's servers are slow or down
• Demo mode: try it without an account
