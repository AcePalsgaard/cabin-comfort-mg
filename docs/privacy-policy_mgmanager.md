# Privacy Policy – Cabin Comfort for MG

Last updated: 22-11-2026

This policy explains how the Android app Cabin Comfort for MG ("the app") handles personal data, in line with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the Danish Data Protection Act (databeskyttelsesloven).

The app is an independent project and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to MG Motor or SAIC Motor.

## 1. Data controller

Christian Münster
Brørup, Denmark
Email: cm@rhea.dk

The developer has no servers of its own and never receives your login or car data. All data the app handles stays on your phone or goes directly between your phone and MG's servers.

## 2. What personal data the app handles

| Data | Why | Where it is stored |
|---|---|---|
| MG iSmart email and password | To log in to your car account | Only on your phone, encrypted with a key from Android Keystore. Excluded from Android backups. |
| Car identifiers and status (VIN, model, battery level, temperatures, climate status) | To show your car's status and send climate commands | Only on your phone |
| Climate profiles and app settings | To remember your preferred settings | Only on your phone |

The app does not collect location data, contacts, photos, advertising IDs or usage statistics. It has no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting services.

## 3. Legal basis (GDPR Article 6)

- **Article 6(1)(b), performance of a contract:** processing your login and car data is necessary to provide the service you ask for, which is controlling your car's climate from the app.
- **Article 6(1)(f), legitimate interest:** storing your settings and profiles on the device so the app works as expected.

## 4. Recipients and data transfers

To control your car, the app sends your login and commands directly to the MG iSmart servers operated by SAIC Motor (for example gateway-mg-eu.soimt.com). These are the same servers the official MG iSmart app uses. SAIC Motor is an independent data controller for the data held on its servers, and its handling of your data is covered by MG's own privacy policy.

Depending on the region you select in the app (Europe, Australia/NZ, Türkiye or Brazil), data may be processed outside the EU/EEA by SAIC Motor. The developer does not transfer your data to anyone.

## 5. Retention

- Your login is kept on your phone until you log out in the app, which deletes it.
- Car data, profiles and settings are kept on your phone until you clear the app's data or uninstall it.
- The developer stores no data, so there is nothing to delete on the developer's side.

## 6. Security

Your password is encrypted on the device using Android Keystore, and all communication with MG's servers uses HTTPS. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, but reasonable measures are taken to protect your data.

## 7. Your rights

Under GDPR you have the right to:

- access the personal data held about you (Article 15)
- have incorrect data corrected (Article 16)
- have your data deleted (Article 17)
- restrict processing (Article 18)
- data portability (Article 20)
- object to processing based on legitimate interest (Article 21)

Because all data is stored only on your phone, you can exercise most of these rights yourself by logging out, clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app. For data held on MG's servers, contact MG/SAIC Motor. For any question about this app, contact the developer at the email above.

## 8. Complaints

You have the right to complain to the Danish Data Protection Agency (Datatilsynet), Carl Jacobsens Vej 35, 2500 Valby, Denmark, www.datatilsynet.dk, or to the data protection authority in your own EU country.

## 9. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly process data about children.
